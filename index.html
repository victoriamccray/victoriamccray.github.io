--- layout: home title: Victoria McCray, MSc --- My name is Victoria, and I'm a research scientist of sorts. My credentials say Masters of Science, but I'm just happy to be done with school for a while. I'm not committed to a single cause per se, but I am committed to change and learning and building. I'm now embracing the opportunities that feel more like me. I've dealt with chronic health issues for most of my life, so I found myself fulfilled in public health until recently. I spent many years in classrooms feeling alone, and so I've felt fulfilled by equitable distribution of educational resources. I've been fascinated with how information is processed for better and for worse in people, technology, and everyday systems for many years. And now after realizing that I myself lived the experience of pain in different forms, I'm interested in ways to build systems for those who experience ongoing pain.
