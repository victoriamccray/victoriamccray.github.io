## Overview

As Director of Finance for a global volunteer-led nonprofit, I designed and documented the operational systems supporting financial management, governance, fundraising, compliance, and organizational reporting. My goal was to transform institutional knowledge into repeatable, scalable processes that could be sustained beyond individual leadership transitions.

The result was a comprehensive operations manual covering financial workflows, governance procedures, sponsorship management, budgeting, compliance, and reporting, enabling future leaders to maintain organizational continuity while improving operational efficiency.

## Responsibilities & Deliverables

This project included the design and documentation of systems for:

- Annual budgeting and financial planning
- Expense approval and reimbursement workflows
- Vendor evaluation and payment operations
- Sponsorship and fundraising management
- Nonprofit governance and compliance
- Board reporting and annual impact reporting
- Risk management and operational documentation
- Leadership transition and knowledge transfer

## Key Systems Developed

### Financial Operations
Designed standardized procedures for budgeting, payment approvals, reimbursements, financial reporting, and vendor management. Established repeatable annual planning processes that balanced organizational priorities with financial sustainability.

### Governance & Compliance
Developed decision frameworks and documentation supporting nonprofit compliance, board approvals, charitable registrations, tax reporting, contractor payments, and organizational policies. Created guidance for navigating complex operational scenarios while maintaining regulatory compliance.

### Fundraising Operations
Created sponsorship workflows, partnership documentation, grant tracking systems, and standardized agreement processes that improved consistency across fundraising activities while reducing administrative burden.

### Reporting & Organizational Learning
Led development of annual impact reporting processes, internal reporting standards, and structured organizational feedback systems that informed strategic planning and continuous improvement.

## System Map

The operations manual was organized around four connected functions, each documented so it could run independently of any one leader:

**Operations Manual** at the center, connected to:
- Financial Operations
- Governance & Compliance
- Fundraising Operations
- Reporting & Learning

## Example Operational Workflows

**Annual Budget Development**
Program Requests → Financial Review → Prioritization → Board Approval → Budget Monitoring → Quarterly Reporting

**Sponsorship Lifecycle**
Identify Partner → Proposal Development → Agreement Review → Payment Processing → Recognition & Reporting

**Payment Operations**
Payment Request → Compliance Review → Approval → Vendor Setup → Payment → Documentation

## Selected Operational Decisions

| Challenge | Solution |
|---|---|
| Ad hoc budget requests | Developed standardized approval policy and documentation requirements |
| Sponsorship negotiations | Created repeatable agreement workflow balancing partnership goals with nonprofit governance |
| International payments | Built decision framework for selecting compliant payment methods |
| Travel support | Designed equitable, application-based funding process |
| Organizational transitions | Developed comprehensive documentation to reduce knowledge loss between leadership teams |

## Design Principles

The documentation was designed around four principles:

- Standardize recurring processes to reduce ambiguity.
- Balance governance with operational flexibility.
- Preserve institutional knowledge through clear documentation.
- Build systems that future volunteers could sustain independently.

## Outcomes

- Standardized financial and governance processes across organizational functions
- Reduced reliance on institutional knowledge through comprehensive documentation
- Created repeatable budgeting, sponsorship, and payment workflows used to support leadership transitions
- Established operational guidance supporting nonprofit compliance and long-term sustainability
